Why we created a gluten-free option

In many churches, navigating gluten intolerance during Communion can be awkward. Separate tables, special requests, or members simply abstaining all break the unity that Communion is meant to foster.

Our gluten-free pre-filled cups solve this completely:

  • They can be distributed in the same tray as standard cups.
  • They are clearly marked but visually similar.
  • They require zero special preparation by volunteers.
  • They are sealed, eliminating any risk of crumb contamination.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it completely safe for celiacs?

Yes. The wafers are produced in a certified gluten-free facility and the cups are sealed, guaranteeing zero contamination during transit and distribution.

What are the ingredients?

The wafer is made from rice flour, corn flour, and water. The juice is 100% natural grape juice.

Can I mix gluten-free and standard in one order?

Absolutely. Most churches order 5-10% of their total volume as gluten-free.

How do congregants know which is which?

The top seal of the gluten-free cup features a distinct, elegant symbol and text so members can confidently select the right cup from the tray.

Does it taste different?

The wafer has a slightly different texture due to the rice and corn blend, but is mild and respectful of the moment. The juice is exactly the same.
